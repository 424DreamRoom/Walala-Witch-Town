Changes by Walala Witch Town, 2026-10-22:
Selected nonverbal Hm?, Hmm... and Huh? recordings. Extracted 125 ms voiced
segments, removed DC offset, converted to mono PCM16 / 44.1 kHz, applied
8 ms fade in and 25 ms fade out, and normalized with a peak cap of 0.70.
Playback varies pitch and volume. These are dialogue syllables, not full
spoken translations of the game's dialogue.
