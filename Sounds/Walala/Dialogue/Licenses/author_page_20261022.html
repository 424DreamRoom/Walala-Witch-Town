500+ Character Voice Audio Files!

Download NowName your own price

Super Dialogue Audio Pack contains more than 500 audio files of human voice over dialogue recorded specifically for video games. The pack includes both male and female voices from 5 different voice actors, and all of the files can be used for commercial purposes (royalty free). Files are 32-bit 44.1 kHz WAV format.

The dialogue is organized into 10 categories:

  1. Completion
  2. Confirmation
  3. Greeting
  4. Farewell
  5. Refusal
  6. Miscellaneous
  7. Damage
  8. Death
  9. Grunting
  10. Shouting
